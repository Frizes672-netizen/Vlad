## Facts (verify before posting)
Vesna Vulović, 22, flight attendant of JAT Flight 367, 26 January 1972; bomb explosion over Czechoslovakia (near Srbská Kamenice); 10,160 m (33,330 ft); 28 on board, she was the only survivor (27 died); reportedly assigned by mistake (confused with another Vesna); pinned in the tail section by a food cart; found by Bruno Honke, a former WWII medic; fractured skull, broken legs, three broken vertebrae, coma for several days, temporarily paralyzed; no memory of the fall; Guinness record for the highest fall survived without a parachute. Note: a 2009 investigation claimed the plane may have been shot down at a lower altitude — the official version is still the record.
