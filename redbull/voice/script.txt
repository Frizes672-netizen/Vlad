He Sued Red Bull for $13 Million!
Imagine buying an energy drink because you believe its advertising.
Red Bull’s famous slogan says, “Red Bull gives you wings!”
But one man decided to ask a very unexpected question…
“Where are my wings?”
Sounds like a joke, right? But listen to this.
In the United States, a man filed a lawsuit against Red Bull, arguing that its advertising suggested benefits in energy and performance that weren’t adequately supported.
And this is where things get interesting.
The case became a class-action lawsuit, and Red Bull agreed to a $13 million settlement.
Yes, thirteen million dollars over an energy drink’s advertising!
But here’s the catch: the money was part of a settlement for eligible class-action claimants. One man didn’t personally walk away with thirteen million dollars.
Red Bull also did not admit wrongdoing as part of the settlement.
Still, this story became legendary.
Because sometimes, all it takes is one determined person to make a massive company face some uncomfortable questions.
And now think about it…
How many advertising promises do we believe without questioning them?
